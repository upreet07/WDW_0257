The Story of Theo and Alicia

The story’s timeline is the trick: Theo’s scenes about discovering his wife Kathy’s affair seem to be happening while he is treating Alicia, but they actually happened six years earlier. Kathy’s lover was Gabriel, Alicia’s husband. After finding out, Theo followed Gabriel home, entered Alicia’s house in disguise, and tied up both spouses. He made Gabriel choose whom to save, and Gabriel chose himself. Theo left them alive, but his betrayal reopened Alicia’s childhood trauma; she shot Gabriel, then fell silent. Years later, Alicia recognized Theo when he joined the hospital, but concealed that she knew him. When she finally told him what happened, she changed the details to show she had identified him. Theo then drugged her to keep her from exposing him and blamed another staff member. Alicia’s hidden diary eventually revealed the truth to the police, making Theo’s careful narration unravel at the end.
